## Verification

Every image above was **opened and visually confirmed** to depict what its filename claims before
being committed. Filenames were not trusted as evidence.

## Rejected candidates

- *FEMA - 30774 - FEMA Community Relations Volunteers* — rejected. It shows federal agency staff in
  FEMA-branded jackets interviewing a resident, not neighbours helping neighbours. PitchIn's whole
  argument is that the town does this itself; an image of a federal response contradicts it.

## Known caveat

`debris-carry.jpg` has a small **AmeriCorps logo** visible on the worker's shirt. In a fictional
Colorado town with fictional squads this is a minor inconsistency. The warm Polaroid treatment
(`sepia`/desaturate) softens it considerably and it is not legible at the sizes used. If it bothers
you, crop the right third of the frame or drop this one slot — nothing else depends on it.

## Reserved slot — not yet filled

`public/photos/ramp-finished.jpg` — **a photograph of a completed residential wheelchair ramp.**

This is the "what the finished project looks like" image, and it is the emotional payoff of the
Duthie ramp after-action report on The Wall. No suitably-licensed photograph of a residential
accessibility ramp was found on Commons; the searches returned public-building ramps in unrelated
countries, which would not read as a neighbour's front porch in Colorado.

**Until a file exists at that path, the photo component renders nothing** — see the spec. There is
no placeholder box and no broken-image icon. Drop a real photo in at that exact filename and it
appears automatically. Any photo of a simple wooden porch ramp works; a personal photo is ideal and
carries no licensing question at all.
